### Naming in the legacy copy of this repository

The legacy folders `Sperm`, `Acrosome Abnormality` and `Boya` correspond, by SHA-256, to the
official `Normal`, `Abnormal` and `Non-sperm`. "Boya" is Turkish for *stain*. The file names
inside the legacy folders are **not** labels: for example, 345 distinct images named
`Normal_*` are officially Abnormal.

## Splits

Stratified 5-fold CV over the images in use (seed 20251005). Inside each outer training
fold, a stratified 15% `val` subset and a 20% `calib` subset are carved out, leaving 65%
for `train`. The tables are versioned in `data/splits/*_splits_v1.csv`. CI checks that no
pixel content appears in two roles of one fold and that every image is tested exactly once.

## Known limitations

* No patient identifiers: images of the same patient can fall in train and test.
* SMIDS non-sperm patches were extracted automatically and some patches are ambiguous, so
  label noise beyond the four detected conflicts is likely.
* Both datasets are single-centre, with one staining protocol each; nothing here supports
  claims about other labs, microscopes or stains.
* Near-duplicates were checked heuristically (identical 64-bit dHash): 4 pairs in SMIDS, none
  across classes. A looser perceptual-hash threshold flagged thousands of cross-class pairs,
  so it is uninformative for these small, similar-looking patches and was not used.
