# Phase 1 - Audit of commit `fa4ada5` (5 Jul 2025)

Every row was checked against the code, the notebook outputs and the **official dataset
releases**: both Mendeley archives were downloaded and verified against Mendeley's published
SHA-256, and every image in the repository was matched to an official file by hash. The
reproducible version of the data checks is `spermtriage data`. Its output is in
[`data/INTEGRITY.md`](../../data/INTEGRITY.md).

Severity: **Critical**: a reported number or claim is false or unsupported. **Major**: it
invalidates or biases an experiment, or creates legal or ethical risk. **Minor**: wrong but
low impact.

## B. Corrections to the prompt pack itself

| Statement in the pack | What the evidence shows |
| --- | --- |
| "The best fully automated results are 93.52% (HuSHeM) and 92.5% (SMIDS)" | Aktas et al. (2025) claim state of the art only over *CNN-based approaches without manual preprocessing*. Their own Table 7 lists Spencer et al. (2022) at 98.2% and Mahali et al. (2023) at 97.6% on HuSHeM. Kılıç (2025, PLOS ONE) reports 96.77% / 96.08%. Published accuracies span 85.2-98.2% (HuSHeM) and 90.2-96.1% (SMIDS) under heterogeneous protocols. |
| "arXiv 2601.10070 has calibration curves for a HuSHeM CNN" | In that paper, "HuSHeM" names a model evaluated on a clinical cohort. Its abstract reports calibration qualitatively and gives no ECE values. |
| "SMIDS fold 1 has 5 exact duplicates" | Correct for byte-identical files. In addition, the official release has 49 pixel-identical pairs, 4 of them cross-class (row 16). |
| Repository facts in Prompt 4 | All confirmed except as refined above. The LFS weights *are* retrievable from GitHub, but they need TensorFlow, which has no stable release for Python 3.14, and they come from the flawed pipeline. They are therefore documented, not characterised. |
